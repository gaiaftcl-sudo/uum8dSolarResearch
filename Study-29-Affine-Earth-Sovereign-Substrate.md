## The shear, in one paragraph

A satellite reentering the atmosphere is a discrete, countable, exactly-ledgerable event: **N objects of known dry mass, reentering at known epochs, on orbits of known inclination.** The field models what that material does after it arrives with **continuous, floating-point chemistry-transport models**, and those models disagree on the **sign** of the ozone response. Ferreira, Huang, Nomura and Wang (*Geophysical Research Letters* 51, e2024GL109280, 2024, doi:10.1029/2024GL109280 — VERIFIED) establish a chlorine-activation mechanism and quantify **mass only**, reporting no ozone percentage. Maloney, Portmann, Ross and Rosenlof (*Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres* 130(6), e2024JD042442, 2025, doi:10.1029/2024JD042442 — REPORTED) run WACCM6 coupled to the CARMA sectional model at a 10 Gg/yr Al₂O₃ flux and report ~1.5 K temperature anomalies, a ~10% reduction in Southern Hemisphere polar-vortex wind speed, and **a weaker springtime ozone hole** — the opposite sign. Revell, Bannister, Brown and colleagues (*npj Climate and Atmospheric Science* 8:212, 2025, doi:10.1038/s41612-025-01098-6 — REPORTED) run SOCOLv4 with prognostic alumina microphysics and find **at most a +0.27% ozone increase at 10 hPa in the Northern Hemisphere**, arising from acceleration of the shallow Brewer-Dobson branch under alumina heating; **their alumina is launch alumina and the paper does not simulate reentry emissions**, offering the result explicitly as a comparison point for satellite reentry ablation. Barker, Marais and colleagues (*Earth's Future*, 2026, doi:10.1029/2025EF007229 — REPORTED, volume and article number not verified) find ozone loss dominated by chlorine from solid propellant with megaconstellation missions at 9%, and report rocket black carbon flipping sign between **+6.47 mW/m² instantaneous** and **−6.40 mW/m² stratospherically adjusted** forcing — one model, one emissions set, two forcing definitions, zero difference in input data. Vliex and colleagues (*Earth's Future*, 2026, doi:10.1029/2025EF007795 — REPORTED) run GEOS-Chem and report **87.7% of the modelled ozone depletion driven by NOx from reentry**, 85.6 mDU annual global column loss, net forcing 4.1 mW/m², with plume chemistry reducing ozone depletion by 17.1% and forcing by 29.1% — a third position on the same dominant-species axis. **Published models disagree on the sign of the ozone response and on the species to which they attribute it, and that disagreement is the shear signal under test.** Whether that spread is attributable to declared admission rules and declared conversion coefficients rather than to any observation is exactly what this court's Act 1 scores, and LOSS (i) is frozen below for the case where it is not. This court re-executes the **counting half** in exact integers — grams and centi-degrees, from one anonymous file — so that whether the arithmetic spread and the physical spread can be told apart becomes a scored question rather than an assumed one. **The claim class, verbatim and load-bearing: a presented configuration is verified; an unknown configuration is not searched.**

## Section zero — the discrimination gate

Before any row is scored, each detector must be shown able to fail. **A gate that cannot fail is not a gate; always-green and always-red are the same defect.** Everything in this section is frozen in this charter **before any admission rule is executed and before any response granule is requested**. ### The four losses, each graded as a loss and published as one

- **LOSS (i) — the flip set is empty.** Every declared admission rule carrying frozen operators, re-executed on the identical frozen rows, ranks every calendar month and every latitude band identically. Then the field's ledger spread is not attributable to declared admission rules, the exact ledger buys nothing here, and the study publishes the census totals with the zero on the page.
- **LOSS (ii) — the confinement census fires everywhere.** Scored form, frozen, with both denominators named: let `a_lo` be the summed anomaly count over the **two** low-load bands and `a_hi` the summed count over the **three** leading bands. The criterion is that the per-band mean in the low-load arms reaches **one quarter** of the per-band mean in the leading bands — cross-multiplied into integers, **LOSS (ii) prints when `6 × a_lo ≥ a_hi`**. The ratio 1/4, the band memberships and the two denominators are frozen here and do not move. A criterion phrased as "an anomaly of the same magnitude" would carry no denominator and could not return NO, which is why it is stated this way.
- **LOSS (iii) — the sealed adversary is not separable on shape.** Scored form, frozen: the confinement concordance computed against a sealed adversary's own month vector must fall **below** the frozen threshold `n/d` while the ledger's own concordance **exceeds** it. If a sealed adversary's month vector clears the same threshold the ledger clears, the two are not separated on shape at this instrument's resolution and LOSS (iii) prints. **Separating on size is forbidden by the program recipe**, so no magnitude comparison may rescue it.
- **LOSS (iv) — a declared rule is not reproducible from its own print.** A rule whose published chain does not close against its own published figures prints **`UNREPRODUCIBLE_FROM_PRINT`**, is excluded from the flip census at the step that does not close, and is published in its own row — never silently reconstructed from a guess. **LOSS (iv) has already fired once**, on Ferreira et al. 2024's population chain; the consequence is stated in full in the admission-rule section below.

#### Crossing R — the read-once quantiser at the archive boundary

The forcing ledger is integer-native on the wire and earns its zero-float property by string census. **The response archives are not**: OMPS-LP extinction, MLS mixing ratios and SAGE III extinction are served as IEEE floats in HDF5 / HDF-EOS5. The house rule holds on both halves only if the crossing is declared, so it is declared here, at the site where it happens, once:

- **Extinction**, served in km⁻¹ → the graded integer is **`floor(value × 10⁹)`**, unit **10⁻⁹ km⁻¹**.
- **Volume mixing ratio**, served dimensionless → the graded integer is **`floor(value × 10¹²)`**, unit **parts per trillion**, which is the substrate's own wire convention for a ratio (`ratio_ppt`, `snr_ppt`, `closure_conc_ppt` in the domain spine above).
- Both are performed **once, at load, on the served array**, with the scale factor frozen and the rounding rule **floor, never round**. No float re-enters after that step, and every threshold comparison, every concordance count and every seal is an integer comparison in the unit named.

**This is the same many-to-one crossing the sensing edge performs**, and the page states it in the same terms: an interval of served values maps to one lattice point, nothing downstream restores the interval, and the honest claim is exactness **at the chosen resolution** with the resolution published as a number a reader checks against the product's own reported precision.

## The exact-integer ledger

### The reentry ledger, and the units artifact it dissolves

**This subsection is measured arithmetic and is published as arithmetic.** It is re-derived by `reproduce/reentry-alumina-ledger.swift` in exact `Int64` rational arithmetic with no float anywhere, and it concerns only what is **consistent** between published figures. It concerns no atmosphere and it is not a finding about anyone's model.

**The yield is an exact rational.** Ferreira et al. 2024's 30 kg of Al₂O₃ per 250 kg satellite is **30/250 = 3/25** exactly. The whole ledger is therefore a counting problem with a rational operator and needs no float at any step.

**Maloney et al. 2025's own stated case, carried through that operator.** More than 60,000 satellites on orbit at a five-year replacement life is **12,000 reentering per year**. At the two representative bus masses:

| Bus dry mass | Reentry mass per year | × 3/25 → alumina per year |
|---|---|---|
| 260 kg | **3,120 t** | **374.4 t** |
| 800 kg | **9,600 t** | **1,152 t** |

**And here is the artifact.** A 10 Gg/yr figure is reachable as reentry **mass** and is not reachable as **alumina** at either representative bus mass: it would require a yield of **10,000/3,120 = 125/39 = 320.5%** at 260 kg and **10,000/9,600 = 25/24 = 104.2%** at 800 kg, both above unity. Only at a 1,250 kg bus (15,000 t/yr of mass) does it become arithmetically possible at all, and then it demands a yield of **2/3** — **50/9 = 5.56×** the 3/25 operator. **So "10 Gg/yr" and "over 360 t/yr" are different quantities, and the ~28× spread that circulates between them is nothing but the ratio of a mass figure to an alumina figure: 10,000/360 = 250/9 = 27.8×.**

**On one unit set the spread is small and exact.** Comparing alumina to alumina at the 800 kg bus: **1,152 / 360 = 16/5 = 3.2×** exactly. And comparing alumina to alumina at the 260 kg bus, the ledger derives **374.4 t/yr** from Maloney's own satellite count against Ferreira's stated "over 360 t/yr" — **374.4 / 360 = 26/25 exactly, so the ledger's figure exceeds Ferreira's by exactly 4%.** Two independently published figures, put on one unit set by an exact rational operator, land 4% apart, where a mass-against-alumina reading of the same two papers lands them 27.8× apart.

**The honest caveat, and it is the whole scope of this subsection.** This arithmetic establishes what is **consistent**, never what any paper **says**. Whether Maloney et al. 2025 label 10 Gg/yr as reentry mass or as alumina still needs confirming against the source text, which did not fetch this pass. **Until it is confirmed, the units reading above is a statement about the arithmetic and not an attribution to that paper**, and it is carried as Open issue 4.

### The confinement analog

**The response must live inside the turning-latitude band the ledger names, and the test is decided in integers with no model, no propagator and no float.**

**The band-assignment rule, reconciled with the bound it sits beside.** The physical statement `|φ| ≤ tc` permits ablation anywhere from the equator to the turning latitude, while the ledger rule `b = floor(tc / 500)` places a row's entire mass in the single slot containing its turning latitude. These are different maps and the charter picks one and says why. **The dwell law is the reason:** for a circular orbit of inclination *i*, the fraction of ground track spent near latitude φ rises without bound as |φ| → *i*, so sub-point density is sharply peaked at the turning latitude and comparatively flat elsewhere. **The frozen assignment is therefore turning-latitude assignment, `b = floor(tc / 500)`, chosen on that argument and stated as a choice.** The alternative — distributing a row's mass over slots 0 … b under a stated dwell weighting — is a different statistic and is named as the arm that would test the choice; it is not instantiated in v1 and is carried as an open issue.

- Over all band-slot pairs (*i*, *j*) with **gᵢ ≠ gⱼ**, count *C* concordant and *D* discordant.

## The Eisenstein framing, and what survives verification

This section is framing rather than evidence, and it is written to the same standard as the rest of the page: what is second-hand is labelled second-hand, what is measured is measured here, and what is an open question is not dressed as a claim.

**The famous ranking is second-hand and is labelled so.** The remark that there have been *only three epoch-making mathematicians — Archimedes, Newton, Eisenstein* is transmitted by **Moritz Cantor**, *Allgemeine Deutsche Biographie* vol. 5 (1877), pp. 774–775, reporting a conversational remark; Cantor was taught by Gauss at Göttingen in 1849–51 but **does not claim to have heard it himself**, and it reached English through E. T. Bell, *Men of Mathematics* (1937). **Nothing in Gauss's own hand contains it.** It is carried here as *reportedly, transmitted by Cantor* and is not load-bearing.

**The firsthand record is better and carries the weight.** Gauss's 1847 preface to Eisenstein's *Mathematische Abhandlungen* secures him *"an honourable place beside his predecessors"* — and the predecessors Gauss **names** are Euler and Lagrange. Gauss to Encke, 23 June 1844: he could be of no help *"by actual teaching, since he clearly has by far surpassed this stage."* In 1845, proposing Dirichlet for the order *pour le mérite*, Gauss noted he had almost proposed Eisenstein instead. (Schappacher, *Mathematics in Berlin*, Birkhäuser 1998, pp. 55–60 — REPORTED.)

**No source attaches a reason to any of it, and this page does not invent one.** What Gauss actually studied is documented: of the fifteen Eisenstein papers in Crelle vol. 27 (1844), he read **one** in detail — the quadratic-reciprocity proof by multiple Gauss sums (Lemmermeyer, arXiv:2101.03344, p. 3 — REPORTED). **And that proof is analytic**: Weil observed it is virtually identical with Gauss's own sixth proof, the Gauss-sum proof. The admired object sits on the analytic side of any line one might try to draw.

**Eisenstein was not an anti-continuity figure, and a framing that made him one would be false.** Eisenstein **series** are holomorphic modular forms on the upper half-plane — functions of a continuous variable whose only discreteness is the lattice they are summed over. He taught analysis in Berlin from 1847 and built an independent analytic theory of elliptic functions the same year. His reciprocity law is a stepping stone in Hilbert's *Zahlbericht* (1897), his lemniscate arithmetic is the source of the Shimura–Taniyama congruence relation, and Eisenstein series are the instrument by which Langlands constructed the **continuous** spectrum of automorphic forms.

**The doctrinal anchor that is true, and it is the sharper one.** Eisenstein's 1847 elliptic-function theory sums **conditionally convergent** series — series whose value depends on the order of summation — and **he fixed the order**. That is Eisenstein summation, it bears his name, and it is exactly the discipline a byte-exact substrate enforces: the delicacy is pushed out of the values and into an order the author fixes and holds. **That, and not a preference for the discrete over the continuous, is the connection this page claims.**

## Reproduce every number on this page

**The ledger census** is reproduced from one anonymous file with one string-level pass.
