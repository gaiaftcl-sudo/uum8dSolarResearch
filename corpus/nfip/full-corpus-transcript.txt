STUDY 42 — THE EXACT CONTRACT
2.7 million flood-insurance settlements graded against the algebra written into the
contracts they settle. Money is carried as native Int128 cents; no Float, no Double
and no float literal touches the data. Int128 crosses a node boundary as a decimal
String and is parsed back, so there is no packing, no endianness and no width shear.
Subject under grading: the arithmetic and the structural integrity of the instrument.
No claimant, community or individual payout decision is assessed anywhere in this work.
