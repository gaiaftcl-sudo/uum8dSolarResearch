## The season's measured record and the instrument's own confession

In July 2026, Japan's Fire and Disaster Management Agency counted **41,174 people transported to hospital by ambulance for heatstroke** in one month, **79 of them confirmed dead**; the week of July 13–19 alone recorded 10,857 transports, the first weekly total above 10,000 since monitoring began May 1 (REPORTED — Japan Times 2026-08-25 and 2026-07-23, quoting FDMA figures). Across the strait, Yangsan, South Gyeongsang Province, reached **42.5 °C at 13:26 KST on 2026-08-02** — the highest temperature measured in Korea's 122 years of modern observation, begun 1904 — after five consecutive days above 40 °C, with more than 20 localities under the country's new top-tier emergency heatwave alert (REPORTED — Korea Times, Al Jazeera, Japan Times, all 2026-08-02, all quoting the Korea Meteorological Administration). Korea's disease agency counted 1,871 heat-related illnesses and 13 deaths between May 15 and July 31; by early August, about 2,220 and 19 (REPORTED — KDCA via AP wire, early August 2026).

Every operational verdict rendered during that record — every alert issued or withheld, every work-stop triggered or not — turned on a **computed** humid-heat number, and the instruments that compute it carry their own published confessions:

- The National Weather Service's operational heat index is a regression. Its own technical attachment, SR 90-23 (Lans P. Rothfusz, 7/1/90), states verbatim: **"No true equation for the Heat Index exists. Heat Index values are derived from a collection of equations that comprise a model."** The document offers what it itself calls "an ersatz version of the Heat Index equation" and closes with: **"Because this equation is obtained by multiple regression analysis, the heat index value (HI) has an error of ±1.3 F."** (VERIFIED — https://www.weather.gov/media/ffc/ta_htindx.PDF fetched and read 2026-08-27; all three quotes verbatim.)
- The most widely used single-expression wet-bulb formula, Stull's (J. Appl. Meteor. Climatol. 50, 2267–2269, 2011), is an empirical fit valid at sea-level pressure only, for RH 5–99% and T −20…+50 °C, mean absolute error ~0.28 °C, error range **−1 °C to +0.65 °C** (REPORTED — band and domain consistently stated by the NCAR NCL `wetbulb_stull` documentation, PMC9941479, and multiple independent calculators; the AMS journal page 403-blocked live fetch this session, so the band is carried as reported wording of a real published figure, not a verbatim quote). The formula itself is VERIFIED from a Stull-authored source — Practical Meteorology, Ch. 4, p. 95, eq. (4.19), read directly.
- The survivability literature puts the stakes in the same unit. Sherwood & Huber (PNAS 107:9552–9555, 2010), verbatim: **"Any exceedence of 35 °C for extended periods should induce hyperthermia in humans and other mammals, as dissipation of metabolic heat becomes impossible."** (VERIFIED — PMC2906879 fetched 2026-08-27.) The chamber work moved the line lower: Vecellio et al. (J. Appl. Physiol. 132:340–345, 2022), 24 young healthy adults at everyday-activity workloads, verbatim: **"Mean Twb,crit values were relatively constant across 36 °C–40 °C humid environments and averaged 30.55 ± 0.98 °C"** and **"all means were significantly lower than the theoretical 35 °C threshold."** (VERIFIED — PMC8799385 fetched 2026-08-27.)

The thesis is one sentence: **at a survivability threshold, the published error band of the operational instrument is verdict-sized** — a formula licensed to be wrong by up to 1.0 °C, graded at a line where 0.98 °C is the empirical standard deviation of the human limit itself, will render EXCEEDS where exact arithmetic renders BELOW, and the reverse, on real archived station-hours. This court computes the thermodynamic wet-bulb temperature T_W as an **exact rational** — bounded root-bracketing on the psychrometric identity, from the integer-tenths (T, T_d, P) triples NOAA's global hourly archive already serves in native integer form — and seals every station-hour where the exact key and each named float instrument render **opposite verdicts** at a frozen threshold, with station id and timestamp. The verdict is about the instrument, never about physiology: **a presented station-hour is verified; a body's response is not modelled.**

## Section zero — the discrimination gate

A flip census that fires on every hour is a turn counter, not a court, and the program has retired an instrument for exactly that defect before. The gate is the study's three loss conditions, each graded as a loss and published as such:

- **The flip ceiling, frozen:** among hours within G of a given threshold, the flip fraction per adversary must be **≤ 1/2** as an exact ratio of integers. A near-threshold census that flips more often than a coin is not reading the float band; it is broken, and the study is void at that threshold.

Every integer in this gate — G = 15 tenths, 0 disagreements, the 1/2 ceiling, the three control rows with their station ids and timestamps — is frozen in this charter before any corpus byte is scored. None of it moves after scoring. A FAIL is published as a FAIL.

The adversaries are separated on **shape, never magnitude**. Stull is not the adversary because its numbers are the wrong size — over most of the chart they are nearly the right size. It is the adversary because it has the wrong shape: a pressure-blind fitted surface standing in for a pressure-dependent thermodynamic root. Rothfusz's shape is a regression over a model table with frozen physiology. The ASHRAE class is the sharpest separation of all: it iterates the **same identity** this court roots on, so the only thing distinguishing it from the exact key is the arithmetic substrate — precisely the object under trial.

**The identity, and the coefficients frozen as exact rationals.** Saturation vapor pressure over water is pinned to the Alduchov–Eskridge relative-error-minimizing Magnus form (named AEKR/AERK in the primary report):

> e_s(t) = **(30547/5000)** · exp( **(141/8)** · t / ( **(6076/25)** + t ) ) hPa, t in °C

— that is 6.1094, 17.625, 243.04, each a finite decimal and hence an exact rational (VERIFIED — the NOAA/DOE primary report, Alduchov & Eskridge, DOE/ER/61011-T6, OSTI 548871, fetched 2026-08-27: Table II gives the form, Table III its maximum relative error 0.384% over −40…+50 °C; journal citation J. Appl. Meteor. 35, 601–609, 1996; the 17.625/243.04 pair independently REPORTED as the minimax choice across secondary dew-point references — the cross-check source read this session is a tertiary reference page, so the tag is REPORTED, while the coefficients themselves stand VERIFIED from the primary report). The moist-air enhancement factor is pinned from the same report, eq. (20): f_w(p) = **(100071/100000)** · exp( **(9/2000000)** · p ), p in hPa, maximum relative error 0.0773% (VERIFIED, same fetch). The psychrometer equation closes the identity: actual vapor pressure e = f_w(P) · e_s(T_d), and T_W is the root of

> F(T_W) = f_w(P) · e_s(T_W) − e − **(653/1000000)** · (1 + **(118/125000)** · T_W) · P · (T − T_W)

with 6.53×10⁻⁴ and 0.000944 the standard Assmann-psychrometer coefficients of the WMO Guide to Instruments and Methods of Observation, WMO-No. 8, CIMO Guide, Chapter 4 (REPORTED — not live-fetched this session; S6 requires them re-read against WMO-No. 8 Annex 4.B and re-frozen verbatim before any scoring, because published psychrometer coefficients span 6.24×10⁻⁴ to 6.7×10⁻⁴ K⁻¹ across configurations and the court must pin exactly one). **The court is scoped to the water branch, T_d > 0 °C:** every frozen threshold sits far above freezing, so the over-ice coefficients are never needed and nothing unverified about ice enters a verdict.

The plurality this court referees is itself measured: a live-fetched comparison paper exhibits **five distinct Magnus-family coefficient sets in simultaneous common use** for the same saturation curve — August-Roche-Magnus, Bolton, Tetens, Buck 1981, Wexler — and states "dozens of formulas have appeared in the literature" (VERIFIED — tc.copernicus.org preprint tc-2023-8, pages 3–5 read directly, 2026-08-27). ## The frozen law (to be sealed at corpus stage)

Drafted here; frozen verbatim, with every integer bound to a named file and station id, before the first station-hour is scored.

**The declared crossings — two, each at its own site, each performed once.**

- **Crossing A — wire integers → exact rationals.** TMP, DEW, SLP arrive as signed scaled integers (tenths) and are read once, at load, as exact rationals with denominator 10. Integer-to-rational: no float parse exists anywhere in the exact arm, because the archive is already integer-native (VERIFIED above).
- **Crossing B — the transcendental, bounded, with the interval ends assigned by sign.** exp is the only non-rational operation in the identity. It is evaluated as a **rational interval** whose end-assignment is frozen sign-correct, because a truncated Taylor sum bounds exp from different sides on the two half-lines. For an argument x ≥ 0: the degree-**32** truncated Taylor sum in exact rationals is the LOWER bound, and that sum plus the frozen geometric tail bound is the upper. For x < 0 — where admitted winter hours live: the Ulsan control's e_s(T_d) argument is −0.654 — the degree-32 even-ending truncated sum is an **UPPER** bound of exp(x), because the first omitted term x³³/33! is negative, and the degree-**33** truncated sum is the LOWER bound. The interval assignment swaps ends by the sign of the argument and by nothing else; both cases are valid because every exponent argument on the admitted domain is bounded by 4 in absolute value. Everything downstream of exp is a rational interval; a comparison is decided only when the whole interval sits on one side.

**The exact-key algorithm, frozen.** For an admitted hour (T, T_d, P), three cases by the sign of T − T_d, each with its own frozen answer. **T > T_d:** compute e = f_w(P)·e_s(T_d) as a rational interval; bracket the root of F on [T_d, T] (F is strictly increasing in T_W on the admitted domain, and F(T_d) < 0 < F(T) holds strictly here, and only here); bisect with exact rational midpoints. **T = T_d** — saturation: fog, RH 100%, exactly the humid-heat hours this court cares about — gives F(T_d) = F(T) = 0 and a degenerate bracket; the frozen answer is **T_W = T exactly**, a rational, with no bisection performed. **T < T_d** — a sensor error the raw archive really contains — inverts the bracket; the hour prints its own exclusion token **`INVERTED_DEWPOINT`**, which is neither absence, nor refusal, nor a verdict, and is published in its own census. **Termination, in integers:** stop when the bracket width is ≤ **1/2000** °C AND every frozen threshold lies strictly outside the bracket — the verdict at every threshold is then a rational comparison. If a threshold remains inside the bracket, refine to width **1/1000000** °C; if it still straddles, print **ON_BOUNDARY** for that (hour, threshold) — its own token, its own census, never folded into EXCEEDS or BELOW. If Crossing B's interval is too wide to decide a bisection sign, the Taylor degree doubles once, to the frozen ceiling 64; an hour undecidable there prints `PRECISION_EXHAUSTED` and is published as such. Same input, same bracket, every machine.

**The float adversaries, frozen as named arms.** Each is evaluated in IEEE-754 binary64, round-to-nearest-even, with the platform libm's `atan`/`exp`, and the libm identity string is recorded in every sealed row — a court whose adversary is float arithmetic records which float arithmetic:

## What this protects — people, ecology, and the planet

**Workers under midday-ban regimes.** Every Gulf state runs a summer outdoor-work ban, and all but one are calendar-and-clock rules: UAE June 15–Sept 15, 12:30–15:00; Saudi Arabia June 15–Sept 15; Kuwait June 1–Aug 31, 11:00–16:00; Bahrain June 15–Aug 31 (REPORTED — MoHRE via Gulf News; Morgan Lewis GCC survey 2026-06). Human Rights Watch, from interviews with 20 migrant workers February–May 2026, reports the clock-based bans "fall short" and the region's one WBGT trigger high and unevenly enforced (VERIFIED — hrw.org, 2026-06-23, fetched live); the finding that extreme Gulf humid heat now peaks in the **evening** and persists overnight (REPORTED — Communications Earth & Environment 5, s43247-024-01763-3) undercuts fixed midday hours structurally. A threshold statute is only as good as the number computed at it; a court that seals the hours where the deployed formula and exact arithmetic sit on opposite sides of the line is the accounting layer under every such rule.

**Heat-warning integrity.** The 2026 season produced, in one region, both failure modes a threshold-gated warning system can have. Korea overhauled its alert system in June 2026 — the new top tier fires on a one-day forecast of apparent temperature ≥ 38 °C or actual ≥ 39 °C, replacing a two-consecutive-day rule — and issued it for the first time on 2026-07-12 (REPORTED — Japan Times 2026-07-12; Seoul Economic Daily). This census has the same comparison **shape** — a computed index against a frozen line — with the arithmetic made exact and public; it is not the same comparison, because Japan's tiers fire on WBGT and Korea's on apparent temperature, and the scoping paragraph above keeps that distinction load-bearing.

**The habitability record.** Raymond et al. 2020 found T_W 35 °C exceedances at least 14 times since 1979 in station data, concentrated at Jacobabad, Ras al-Khaimah, Jeddah, and Dhahran, and a doubling of extreme humid-heat frequency since 1979 (REPORTED — Science Advances 6:eaaw1838; the study used HadISD, a QC'd derivative of the same NOAA ISD archive this court reads). ## What this court does not judge

- **Physiology.** The court computes a thermodynamic property of air from three archived integers. It does not model sweating, acclimatization, clothing, workload, age, or any body's response. The claim class, verbatim and load-bearing: **a presented station-hour is verified; a body's response is not modelled.**
- **Heat-stress science.** Nothing here claims exact arithmetic replaces heat-stress modelling, chamber studies, or epidemiology. The 35 °C ceiling is Sherwood & Huber's; the 30.55 °C mean is Vecellio's, carried with its authors' own humid-regime caveat and their finding that hot-dry critical values are far lower still (25.75–27.82 °C at low vapor pressures — VERIFIED). The court grades instruments at the field's published lines; it neither moves nor endorses them.
- **Medical or occupational advice.** This page recommends nothing to any worker, employer, agency, or patient. It is research output: a bookkeeping study on public archived weather bytes.
- **WBGT as observed.** Natural wet-bulb plus globe is an instrument reading with siting, wind, and radiation in it, separated from psychrometric T_W by ISO 7243's own annex (VERIFIED, quoted above). The court grades computations, and only ever the computation it names.

The frozen |elevation| ≤ 50 m admission bound keeps the SLP-for-station-pressure approximation bounded, and the corpus-stage upgrade path is named with it: read ISD's MA1 station-pressure field where present, and grade from the station's own air.
- **Psychrometric T_W is itself a convention.** The psychrometer equation's root and the adiabatic-saturation temperature are close but not identical objects; the exact key is exactly the frozen identity above, no more. The adversaries are graded against the same identity (ITER) or their own published definitions (STULL) — no arm faces a moving target.
- **ISD QC and siting are inherited, not adjudicated.** The admission set is frozen and every exclusion published with its characters, but upstream QC, station siting, instrument changes, and archive revisions are NOAA's; HadISD exists precisely because raw ISD needs care (REPORTED). - **ROTHFUSZ cannot flip at a T_W threshold and is not pretended to.** It outputs a heat index in °F; its census is a deferred act with thresholds frozen before scoring. What stands now is its documentary role: the operational instrument's own statement that no exact equation exists.
- **A flip is not a harm.** A sealed flip-hour proves the instruments disagree at the line on that hour's archived air — nothing about what any person experienced or decided. S4's window intersection establishes stake, not causation, and the page will not conflate them.
