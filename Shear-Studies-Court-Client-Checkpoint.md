## What the replay found, and what it changed

**Six fixtures were stale, and the court said so by name.** The 2026-08-26 walk posted two fields to
`eclipse`, `seismic`, `eht`, `qcd`, `health` and `cs`; today each of those courts answers
`REFUSED_NO_INSTANCE: this study grades on <fields>; missing <fields>` — the grader table is generated
from the grader bodies, so the court names exactly what it reads. Four of the six were repaired in the
fixture from the refusal alone (eclipse → gnss with `confinement_ppm`/`lag_s`; seismic → treaty with
`pn_pred_s`; eht → experimentalist with all six; qcd → jet_analyst with `color_isolated`) and now
answer LIVE. **A refusal that names the missing field is the instrument working**; an older fixture
that still passed would have meant the court had stopped reading.

**Go First Dice wanted arrays, not comma strings.** `die_a…die_e` and `face_counts` are read with an
integer-list parser over a JSON array; the comma-string form answered `REFUSED_CITED_MISMATCH`. The
fixture carries arrays of decimal strings and the study answers LIVE.

**Two courts cannot be reached through the `math_court` tool today — that is the finding, not the
fixture.** `health` grades on `arm_counts` and `cs` grades on `num2`/`den2`, and both fields were posted;
the tool's forwarder copies only the keys in its `courtBodyKeys` list into the court body, and those
keys are not on it, so the court sees them as absent. The REST ingest carries them; the MCP face does
not. The repair (the forwarder derives its key set from the catalog's `may_ingest` plus the grader
table, so a court cannot read a field the tool cannot carry) is **committed at HEAD and not yet
serving**; both rows read LEARN here until the next binary roll, and this page is re-run then.

**Twelve studies have no court on this surface.** 03, 04, 05, 08, 10, 15, 26, 27, 29, 30 are OPEN or
charter-only on the board and have never had a domain; 28 (wet-bulb) and 32 (taxi-out) are SEALED on
local gates and their courts are not on the MCP. They are listed, not omitted.
