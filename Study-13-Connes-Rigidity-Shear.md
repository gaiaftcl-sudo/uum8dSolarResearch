## For every reader

Connes's rigidity conjecture asks whether certain discrete groups have **rigid** structure in their operator algebras. The classical formulation lives in continuous von Neumann algebras. UUM-8D projects group structure into **Jordan-bonded linking invariants** on the Eisenstein lattice — two integers \((q,r)\).

## What the WIN means

Words that are the same group element after relators share a linking coordinate. Continuous spectral radius is offset from that coordinate on every row. Rigidity is the discrete class, not a float gap.
